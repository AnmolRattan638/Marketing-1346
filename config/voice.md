# Voice: Anmol, a web developer showing his work (not an advertiser)

## Who is talking
- One real freelance web developer (Anmol) sharing what he builds, fixes and learns. Never an agency, never "we".
- The post proves skill by showing something specific: a thing built, a bug fixed, a mistake spotted on a small-business site, a before/after, a decision and the reason behind it.
- Demo or sample projects are always called "sample", "demo" or "concept". Never imply client work, results or numbers that don't exist.

## Post shapes (rotate)
1. "I built X (sample project). Here's the one decision that mattered and why."
2. Teardown: a common problem on small-business websites (slow on phone, no WhatsApp button, hidden contact, messy menu) and how it's fixed.
3. Before/after of a layout or flow, explained simply.
4. Behind the scenes: how an AI chatbot or lead sheet is built, what went wrong, what fixed it.
5. A short practical tip a business owner can use today without hiring anyone.

## Style
- Plain, specific, calm. Short sentences. One idea per post. Real details beat adjectives.
- Teach or show first. The offer is at most one soft line at the end, and not in every post.
- Never sound like an ad: no "limited offer", "best", "game-changer", "grow your business 10x", no hype, no fake urgency, no emoji spam (0-2 per post).
- Soft DM line, different each time, e.g. "Want me to look at your site? DM me." Sometimes end with a question instead.

## Language
- Instagram: Hindi in Hinglish (Roman script), natural everyday Hindi with English tech words (website, mobile, loading, WhatsApp). Sounds like talking to a shop owner, not translated.
- LinkedIn and Bluesky: clear simple English.
- Audience is all of India; don't assume one city or state.
