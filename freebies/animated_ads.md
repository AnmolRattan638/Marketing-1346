# 20-second ad: what it must show, in order

- [ ] Seconds 0 to 3: the customer's problem in one picture and one line
- [ ] Seconds 3 to 8: your business solving it, shown not described
- [ ] Seconds 8 to 15: one clear detail (a real product, place or step)
- [ ] Seconds 15 to 20: one next step (call, WhatsApp or visit) on screen and spoken
- [ ] Text is big enough to read on a phone with the sound off
- [ ] Your name and area appear once, at the end
- [ ] One idea only; everything else is cut
