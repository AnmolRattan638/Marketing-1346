# Website checklist: 10 things a customer judges in 5 seconds

- [ ] Opens in under 3 seconds on a mobile phone
- [ ] Your business name, city and what you do are visible without scrolling
- [ ] A call or WhatsApp button that works with one tap
- [ ] Real photos of your place or work, not stock pictures
- [ ] Timings and location with a Google Maps link
- [ ] Prices or a clear 'ask for a quote' line
- [ ] Looks the same on a small phone and a laptop
- [ ] A short page about who you are (customers trust a face)
- [ ] Reviews or a link to your Google reviews
- [ ] Your website address is on your Instagram bio, Google listing and visiting card
