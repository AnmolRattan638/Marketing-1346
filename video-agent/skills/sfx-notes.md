# SFX notes

Adapted from the suggest-sfx skill in hassancs91/claude-youtube-editor (MIT). Only the taste rules are
kept. Its ElevenLabs generation and user-audit steps are not used here.

- Think function first. Ask what the moment needs, then pick the sound:
  motion at a cut -> whoosh, tension before a reveal -> riser, emphasis on a word -> pop, small snap -> click.
- Less is more. A few well-placed effects beat one on every beat. Texture that could be dropped is
  better dropped.
- Sync to the word, not roughly near it. A pop lands on the first sound of the word it emphasises.
- Layer only the biggest moment of the video: riser then pop on the key word.
- Effects sit under the voice. Short-form narration is almost continuous, so effects need to be
  clearly louder than they feel necessary, but never louder than the voice.
